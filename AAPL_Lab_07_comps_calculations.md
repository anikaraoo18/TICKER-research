# AAPL Lab 07 — Comparable-Company Calculations

## Scope and conventions

- Valuation date: **September 9, 2026** regular-session closing price.
- Qualified peers only: **MSFT, GOOGL, DELL, META, and HPQ**.
- LTM diluted EPS is the sum of the four most recent reported **GAAP diluted EPS** quarters available on or before September 9, 2026. It is not calculated from net income and a point-in-time share count.
- Dollar amounts are USD per share. Minor differences from annual EPS may arise because reported quarterly EPS is rounded.
- **EV/EBITDA is unavailable.** Uniform reported GAAP EBITDA has not been established for all companies, and GOOGL, DELL, and META also have unresolved multi-class market-capitalization treatment. No EV/EBITDA is forced.

## Peer-median P/E and AAPL implied price

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Qualified-peer P/E observations | MSFT 27.37x; GOOGL 16.61x; DELL 31.12x; META 24.63x; HPQ 12.41x |
| **Peer-median P/E** | **24.63x** |
| AAPL LTM diluted EPS | $8.71 |
| **AAPL implied share price = 24.63 × $8.71** | **$214.53 per share** |

This is a mechanical relative-valuation output from the documented qualified-peer median. It is not a recommendation. EV/EBITDA remains unavailable and is not used.

HPQ is removed from this test because its PC/printing mix and margin structure are a stated qualification concern. The test does not change the designated qualified-peer set; it shows the effect of that single peer-screening change.

## Interpretation and limitations

AAPL’s September 9, 2026 market price was **$315.34 per share**, compared with the base P/E-implied value of **$214.53 per share**. The market price was **$100.81 per share (46.99%) above** the base implied value.

This is a mechanical relative-valuation comparison, not a recommendation. Results are limited by the qualified peers’ differing business models and by EV/EBITDA being unavailable because uniform EBITDA and multi-class market-capitalization treatment remain unresolved.
