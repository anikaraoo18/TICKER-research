# Project 1 Decision Memo: Apple Inc. (`AAPL`)

**Decision user:** Investment committee

**Recommendation:** **Watch/Defer.** Do not initiate from this evidence set; this is not investment advice.

## Evidence and market-price context

The evidence supports separate method results, not one precise target price or an average. The FCFF DCF discounts a CFO-less-capex FCFF proxy at WACC and bridges enterprise value to equity. The five-year pro-forma projects three statements and FCFE before share repurchases, discounted at cost of equity. These architectures are reported side by side, not silently merged.

The P/E and FCFF comparisons use the September 9 price because those exercises were built with that convention. The pro-forma uses the latest completed close available during its September 24 review, September 23. Edition A identifies September 1, 2026 as its valuation date. The comparisons therefore use different dates and are not presented as one aligned range.

## Material limitations

- The FCFF starting value is a CFO-less-capex proxy, not a fully reconciled FCFF measure. Cash classification, the enterprise-to-equity bridge, and the diluted-share convention require further support.
- The pro-forma holds marketable securities, debt, debt rate, and diluted shares fixed; it uses a 10.0% cost of equity, 2.5% terminal growth, and residual cash repurchases. Terminal value is 70.53% of base value.
- The P/E peers differ from Apple in business model and fiscal-year timing. The EV/EBITDA result relies on Garmin alone and uses a cash-only bridge that excludes marketable securities.
- Gross margin is the larger tested Lab 11 driver ($5.66 per-share span versus $4.19 for Mac growth). A Services downside, including regulatory pressure on Google search payments, has not been modeled.

## Committee action and monitoring triggers

Maintain **Watch/Defer**. Before considering initiation, require a reconciled FCFF build and cash policy, a common valuation-date convention, and a documented Services-downside case. Reconsider if a reconciled FCFF model materially raises its defensible range, updated filing evidence changes Services growth or gross-margin support, regulatory developments impair Services economics, or the market price moves into a method range after assumptions and dates are refreshed.
