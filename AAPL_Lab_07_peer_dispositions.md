# AAPL Lab 07 — Peer Dispositions

## Frozen peer-selection policy

| Dimension | Inclusion logic | Exclusion / qualification logic |
|---|---|---|
| Business model and revenue drivers | Public operating companies with meaningful consumer-technology revenue from devices, software, services, or a comparable ecosystem; diversified revenue is preferred over a single product line. | Exclude ETFs, funds, banks, insurers, REITs, shells, telecom carriers, retailers, and pure component suppliers. Qualify companies that are primarily enterprise software, semiconductors, or gaming because their demand drivers differ from Apple’s. |
| Geography / regulation | Companies with substantial revenue exposure to developed global consumer markets and material exposure to technology-platform, privacy, app-store, trade, or antitrust regulation. | Qualify companies concentrated in one country or exposed to materially different reporting, currency, or regulatory environments. |
| Scale and growth | Established companies with multi-year operating history, positive revenue, and sufficient scale to support a stable LTM multiple. Prefer companies with growth supported by product, services, ecosystem, or recurring-revenue drivers. | Exclude early-stage, distressed, or turnaround firms. Qualify companies materially smaller or faster-growing than Apple because their multiples may embed different expectations. |
| Margins / capital intensity | Positive EBITDA and net income; a business model with hardware, services, R&D, and capital requirements that can be compared to Apple’s mix. | Qualify asset-light software firms, highly capital-intensive manufacturers, or companies with substantially different margin structure. |
| Leverage / risk | Normal operating liquidity and debt levels that do not dominate enterprise value; no major going-concern, restructuring, or unusual one-time event. | Exclude financially distressed firms. Qualify companies whose leverage, litigation, regulatory exposure, or concentration risk materially differs from Apple’s. |
| Metric availability / definition | LTM or most recently reported EBITDA, EBIT, net income, debt, cash, diluted shares, and market value are available from primary filings or a documented market-data source. Use consistently defined reported or adjusted metrics across all accepted peers. | Reject peers without enough current data to calculate consistent EV/EBITDA or P/E. Do not mix LTM with forward metrics, adjusted with reported metrics, or fiscal periods without a clear qualification. |
| Market and reporting dates | Use market prices as of the same valuation date as the AAPL DCF or the nearest documented trading date; use each company’s latest available LTM financials. | Qualify stale filings, materially different fiscal periods, or prices measured on a different date. Record every as-of date..... |
