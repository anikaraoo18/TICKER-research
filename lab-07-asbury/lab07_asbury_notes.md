## My peer explanation
AutoNation is a usable peer because it shares Asbury’s franchised vehicle-retail model and service/parts economics. Group 1 is qualified rather than treated as identical because it also operates franchised dealerships but has a different geographic and business mix, which can affect its P/E multiple. These peers provide a reference for Asbury; they do not prove Asbury is fairly valued.


## My changed-peer explanation
Group 1 has the higher P/E multiple (11.450149x), while AutoNation’s P/E is lower (10.037825x). Removing Group 1 leaves only AutoNation, so Asbury’s implied value falls from the two-peer median of $231.00 to $215.81, a decrease of $15.18. With one peer remaining, the result is a reference estimate rather than a peer range, so information is lost.
