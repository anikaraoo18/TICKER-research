## Initial thesis

**Preliminary recommendation: Watch/Defer.**

Apple reported fiscal 2025 total net sales growth of 6%, while Services net sales grew 14% and generated a 75.4% gross margin. However, the evidence collected so far does not establish an intrinsic-value range or compare that range with the market price as of the valuation date. The committee should therefore defer an initiation decision until operating cash flow, reinvestment, capital-cost, and enterprise-to-equity assumptions are independently modeled and stress-tested.

### Filing interpretation notes

- **Cash policy:** $35,934 million is reported cash and cash equivalents. The valuation bridge will not automatically add the entire amount until a defensible operating-cash requirement is assessed. Current and non-current marketable securities are recorded separately and will be evaluated separately rather than silently included as cash.
- **Debt policy:** Reported interest-bearing debt is provisionally $98,657 million, calculated as $7,979 million commercial paper + $12,350 million current term debt + $78,328 million non-current term debt. Lease liabilities and other debt-like obligations require separate policy decisions and sourcing before they enter the final bridge.
- **Share-count convention:** The FY2025 diluted weighted-average count of 15,004.697 million shares is the provisional valuation denominator because it reflects the EPS dilution convention in the filing. It differs from the 14,773.260 million point-in-time common shares outstanding at fiscal year-end; the final model will state whether and how that timing difference is reconciled.
- **Source quality check:** All reported facts above reconcile to Apple’s FY2025 primary 10-K. The debt total is a calculated output from separately reported balance-sheet lines, not a separately reported line item.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Services can maintain growth that is sufficient to remain a material contributor to Apple’s consolidated sales and margin mix; this will be tested against product mix, competition, regulation, and segment disclosures.
2. A mature-company FCFF model can use a sustainable long-run revenue-growth, operating-margin, reinvestment, and cost-of-capital framework; each input remains unestimated in this Edition A.
3. The final enterprise-to-equity bridge will separately classify reported cash, marketable securities, interest-bearing debt, lease liabilities, and any other senior or non-common claims rather than relying on a single net-debt figure.

## Unknowns and uncertainties

1. The market price and implied market valuation as of September 1, 2026 have not yet been captured; therefore, no conclusion about upside or downside is supported.
2. The durability of Services growth, product demand, and regional demand - especially Greater China - has not been forecast or stress-tested.
3. The amount of cash required for operations, treatment of marketable securities, lease liabilities, and the appropriate as-of share count remain unresolved bridge choices.

## Research and validation plan

1. Reconcile revenue, EBIT, depreciation and amortization, capital expenditures, and operating working-capital changes to the 10-K, including units and periods.
2. Identify non-recurring items and accounting-basis changes in the filing notes; decide whether each is reported, normalized, or retained in the forecast base.
3. Build an FCFF forecast with explicit operating-driver assumptions and a documented cost of capital.
4. Reconcile enterprise value to common-equity value with separately sourced cash, debt, debt-like claims, noncommon claims, and diluted shares.
5. Validate the conclusion through a reverse-DCF implication and a comparable-company or market-multiple cross-check; explain disagreements rather than averaging them away.
6. Define a falsification test: identify the observable evidence that would require the committee to revise or withdraw the thesis.
