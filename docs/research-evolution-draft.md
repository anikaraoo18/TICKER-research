### What changed from Edition A to Edition B, and why?

Edition A had not established an intrinsic-value range. Edition B adds the
documented Lab 06 FCFF DCF, Lab 08 P/E, Lab 10/11 five-year pro-forma and
sensitivity results, and the AAPL/GRMN single-peer EV/EBITDA cross-check. The
evidence supports retaining Watch/Defer because the
methods remain separate, the market-price observations are not on one common
date, and material model limitations remain: the FCFF proxy and cash treatment,
peer comparability, fixed pro-forma assumptions, and an unmodeled Services
downside. This later update does not alter the preserved Edition A.
