## Cathie Wood threshold probe

COST increased revenue in all 4 year-over-year comparisons. At thresholds of 2 of 4, 3 of 4, and 4 of 4, COST passed each time. The saved threshold remains 3 of 4.

I kept 3 of 4 because it requires sustained growth in most comparisons and is stricter than allowing only 2 of 4 increases. This probe shows that a threshold is a design choice; for COST, this particular threshold range did not change the verdict.

## Partner discussion

My partner asked why Cathie Wood passed NVIDIA when the partner would not necessarily expect that result.

I explained that the Cathie Wood screen used only three reported 10-K checks: revenue grew from first to last year, revenue increased in at least 3 of 4 year-over-year comparisons, and operating cash flow was positive every year.

I explained that a pass is not a claim that the real Cathie Wood would buy NVIDIA; it is a limited, reproducible screen that does not evaluate TAM, technology, competition, or a five-year outlook.

After the bull/bear exchange, my partner agreed that the Buffett and Cathie Wood passes make NVDA worth further review, but Burry's failed cash-flow-quality check should be investigated in the 10-K before treating the panel as support for an investment decision.
