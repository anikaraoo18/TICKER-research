## Apple presentation

I selected Apple because its public filings provide enough detail to model its Devices and Services business. My pro-forma DCF value was $118.12 per fixed diluted share, compared with the recorded $337.02 market close on September 23, 2026. I concluded watch/defer, not a buy or sell recommendation, and did not average conflicting valuation methods.

In Lab 11, gross margin had the larger value-per-share span over my tested ranges ($5.66 versus $4.19 for Mac-category revenue growth). My partner asked about revenue mix, gross-margin change, and repurchases. I referred to Apple’s FY2025 10-K: Products and Services Performance for mix, MD&A for the margin change, and the cash-flow statement and assumption table for repurchases. The partner found the segment-level valuation build and the high-margin Services-versus-Products connection clear. The suggested improvement was a downside Services case, including slower growth or regulatory pressure on Google search payments.

## Partner explanation back

My partner summarized my Apple conclusion as watch/defer rather than a buy or sell recommendation. They identified gross margin as the main driver over my stated sensitivity ranges and identified the model simplifications and the unmodeled Services downside case as important limitations. I agreed with that summary.

## NVIDIA review

I asked why NVIDIA was selected, what supports its main driver, how revenue growth reaches value, and whether the sensitivity ranking depends on the ranges. My partner cited FY2026 Compute & Networking revenue of $193,479M out of $215,938M total revenue in NVIDIA’s FY2026 Form 10-K MD&A and Note 17. My check ($193,479M / $215,938M) was about 89.6%, supporting the approximately 90% claim.

Revenue growth flows through gross profit, operating profit, FCFE, and terminal value; its ranking is conditional on the stated ranges. I concluded that NVIDIA depends heavily on AI-driven Compute & Networking growth. Revenue growth was the largest driver over the stated ranges, while the wide +/-1,000-basis-point range and 84% terminal-value share are key limitations. Its causal chain from revenue to FCFE and value was clear; I recommended a more conservative revenue-growth range, which my partner agreed to consider.
