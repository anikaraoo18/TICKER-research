## Script

### Company and decision - 0:20 to 0:50

Apple is the company under review, and the intended user is an investment committee deciding whether to initiate, watch or defer, or not initiate. The current conclusion is Watch/Defer because the available methods provide separate evidence rather than one reconciled valuation.

### Main assumptions - 0:50 to 1:45

The FCFF model begins with a CFO-less-capex proxy, so it is not a fully reconciled FCFF measure. The pro-forma uses revenue growth of 4.5%, 4.0%, 3.5%, 3.0%, and 2.5%, a 46.5% gross margin, a 17.0% tax rate, a $30.0 billion cash floor, fixed debt and diluted shares, a 10.0% cost of equity, and 2.5% terminal growth. These are documented model assumptions, not Apple guidance.

### Valuation evidence - 1:45 to 2:45

The FCFF DCF sensitivity range is $65.66 to $103.95, with an $80.1646 base value. The five-year pro-forma base is $118.12, and the one-at-a-time sensitivity range is $115.29 to $120.94. The two-peer P/E range is $153.38 to $236.38. The single-peer Garmin EV/EBITDA cross-check implies $230.37 per Apple diluted share. These are separate methods and are not averaged.

### Risks and method disagreement - 2:45 to 3:45

The valuation methods disagree, and their market-price comparisons use different stated dates. The FCFF and cash treatment need reconciliation. The peer analyses have business-model and fiscal-period differences, and the EV/EBITDA result relies on one peer. Gross margin was the larger tested sensitivity driver, with a $5.66 span compared with $4.19 for Mac growth. A Services downside, including regulatory pressure on Google search payments, has not been modeled.

### Committee action and reversal triggers - 3:45 to 4:40

My action is Watch/Defer. Before considering initiation, I would require a reconciled FCFF and cash policy, a common valuation-date convention, and a documented Services-downside case. I would reconsider if a reconciled FCFF model materially raised the defensible range, filing evidence changed Services growth or gross-margin support, regulation impaired Services economics, or the market price moved into a method range after inputs and dates were refreshed.

## Closing - 4:40 to 5:00

The current evidence supports continued monitoring, not a buy or sell conclusion. That is why my recommendation remains Watch/Defer.
