## Business model

Apple designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, accessories, and related services. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, p. 1.**

In FY2025, Apple generated $416.2 billion of total net sales. iPhone was the largest category at $209.6 billion, followed by Services at $109.2 billion; Mac, iPad, and Wearables/Home/Accessories generated the remaining sales. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 23.**

Services includes, among other activities, advertising, the App Store, and cloud services. The filing reports that Services revenue grew because of higher sales from those activities. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 23.**

Management attributes the Greater China decline primarily to lower iPhone sales, partly offset by higher Mac sales. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 22.**

## What is working

Services is Apple’s central operating-value driver in this report: it grew faster than consolidated sales and carried a much higher reported gross margin than products. **Source: Analyst inference from Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 23-24.**

The company also maintained substantial financial resources. Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $132.4 billion at September 27, 2025, and management states that these resources, operating cash generation, and access to debt markets should be sufficient for cash needs and the capital-return program over the next 12 months and beyond. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 25.**

During FY2025, Apple repurchased $89.3 billion of common stock and paid $15.4 billion of dividends and dividend equivalents. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 25.**

## What needs attention

Apple’s product gross-margin percentage fell in FY2025. Management attributes the decline primarily to product mix and tariff costs, partly offset by other favorable costs. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 24.**

Operating expenses grew 8% to $62.2 billion, including a 10% increase in research and development to $34.6 billion. Management attributes R&D growth primarily to higher headcount-related and infrastructure-related costs. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 24.**

Apple’s FY2025 effective tax rate was 15.6%, compared with 24.1% in FY2024. Management says the lower rate included a $10.7 billion year-over-year decrease in tax provision related to the State Aid Decision, among other factors. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 24.**

The report does not assume that the FY2025 tax rate is sustainable. **Source: Author research judgment - UNSOURCED.**

## Financial position and valuation bridge inputs

Apple reported $35.9 billion of cash and cash equivalents at September 27, 2025. It also reported $18.8 billion of current marketable securities and $77.7 billion of non-current marketable securities, which are separate line items from cash. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 31.**

Reported interest-bearing debt was $98.7 billion on a carrying-value basis: $8.0 billion of commercial paper, $12.4 billion of current term debt, and $78.3 billion of non-current term debt. **Source: Calculation from Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 31; Note 9 - Debt, pp. 43-44.**

Apple used 15,004.697 million weighted-average diluted shares to calculate FY2025 diluted earnings per share; 14,773.260 million shares were outstanding at fiscal year-end. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 3 - Earnings Per Share, p. 36; Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 31.**

The final valuation bridge will separately assess operating cash, marketable securities, lease liabilities, and other debt-like or non-common claims rather than treating the reported debt figure as a complete net-debt answer. **Source: Author research judgment - UNSOURCED.**

## Risks and challenges

Management identifies tariffs and other trade measures as a risk to supply chains, raw-material and component availability, pricing, and gross margin. The eventual impact is described as uncertain. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 21.**

The decline in Greater China sales and the dependence of that decline on lower iPhone sales make regional iPhone demand a material research issue. **Source: Analyst inference from Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 22.**

At September 27, 2025, Apple also had $16.8 billion of fixed lease-payment obligations, $56.2 billion of manufacturing purchase obligations, and $14.8 billion of other purchase obligations. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 25.**

## Management outlook

Item 7 provides no numerical revenue, margin, or earnings forecast. **Source: Review of Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 21-26.**

Management’s qualitative outlook is cautious: macroeconomic conditions, exchange rates, tariffs, product mix, and costs may affect results and future gross margins. **Source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 21 and 24.**
